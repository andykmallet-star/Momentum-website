Fractional COO

fCOO Services

Embedded operational leadership for your business

We support businesses by being embedded within the organisation — providing advice, support, and consultancy based on lived experience and a proven track record of operational oversight, delivery and transformation. A Fractional COO gives you access to senior operational leadership precisely when and where you need it, without the cost or commitment of a full-time hire.

  • Embedded operational oversight and senior advisory support
  • Board and Management Committee representation and support
  • Operational Excellence and efficiency improvement programmes
  • Risk management, governance frameworks and regulatory compliance
  • People, process and platform optimisation across service lines
  • Interim COO cover and senior leadership continuity
  • Strategic planning and operational roadmap development

Mergers & Acquisitions

M&A Support & IMO

Full deal-cycle support, separation and integration execution

We provide support across the full M&A deal cycle — from pre-deal due diligence through to separation planning and hands-on integration execution. Where an Integration Management Office (IMO) is required, we can establish and run it end-to-end, providing the senior operational leadership to drive your integration or separation forward.

  • Pre-deal support, due diligence and deal structuring input
  • Separation planning and carve-out execution
  • IMO design, establishment and end-to-end leadership
  • Integration execution and Day 1 readiness planning
  • Synergy identification, tracking and post-deal value realisation
  • Regulatory and compliance risk management throughout the deal

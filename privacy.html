1.About this statement

Momentum Advisory (“Momentum”, “we”, “us”) is a Jersey-based management consultancy serving regulated financial services businesses. Momentum Advisory is the trading name of Andy Mallet, a sole trader based in Jersey.

This statement explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we look after it and the rights you have. It applies if you:

  • visit our website or download one of our free resources;
  • are a client, a prospective client, or work for one;
  • are an introducer, referral partner or professional contact;
  • take part in a meeting with us; or
  • work with us as an associate, subcontractor or supplier.

It does not cover personal information we handle on behalf of our clients while delivering an engagement. In that situation the client decides how the information is used, and the client’s own privacy notice applies. Section 8 explains how we handle that information.

2.Who is responsible for your information

Momentum is the controller of the personal information described in this statement. We comply with the Data Protection (Jersey) Law 2018 and, where they apply to the people we deal with, the UK General Data Protection Regulation and the EU General Data Protection Regulation.

We are registered with the Jersey Office of the Information Commissioner as a controller, under registration number 104183.

Andy Mallet, our Founder and Principal Consultant, is responsible for data protection at Momentum. You can contact him at info@momentumjersey.com.

5.Our use of artificial intelligence

Momentum is an AI-native consultancy. We use a small number of approved AI tools to support research, analysis, drafting and note-taking. We govern this through our AI Acceptable Use Policy, which is available on request. In particular:

  • People stay accountable. Every piece of work we deliver is reviewed and validated by our consultant, who remains responsible for it.
  • No automated decisions about you. We do not use AI to make decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on individuals, and we do not use it to profile people.
  • Your information is not used to train AI models. Our approved tools are configured, or contractually committed, not to use our content for model training.
  • Client personal information is protected. Personal information we handle for clients is processed only in tools covered by appropriate data processing terms.

6.Meeting transcription

With your agreement, we may use an AI note-taking tool to transcribe meetings so that we can keep accurate notes. The tool we use runs on our own device and does not join the meeting as a visible participant. It does not keep an audio recording: audio is deleted once transcription is complete, and the transcript and notes are stored in Europe.

We will always ask at the start of a meeting before using it. If anyone present objects, we will not use it. Transcripts and notes are kept with the related client or prospect record and deleted in line with section 11.

7.Our website and cookies

Our website uses only the cookies that are strictly necessary for it to work. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Our website host records technical information such as IP addresses to keep the site secure and running. When you submit a form, the details you provide are processed by our website host and the resource you requested is sent to you by our email delivery provider.

If we introduce analytics or other non-essential cookies in future, we will update this statement and ask for your consent where required.

8.When we act on behalf of our clients

During engagements we may see personal information that belongs to our clients, such as information about their staff, customers or, for some clients, other individuals connected with their business. In these cases the client is the controller and Momentum acts as its processor. We:

  • use that information only on the client’s documented instructions and for the purpose of the engagement;
  • keep it confidential and protect it with appropriate security measures;
  • do not enter it into AI tools that are not covered by appropriate data processing terms;
  • do not use it for our own purposes; and
  • return or delete it at the end of the engagement, unless the client instructs otherwise or the law requires us to keep it.

If you want to exercise your rights over that information, please contact the client organisation. If you contact us, we will pass your request to them promptly.

10.International transfers

Some of our providers hold information outside Jersey. Transfers to the United Kingdom and the European Economic Area are permitted because those jurisdictions are recognised as providing adequate protection. Where information is transferred elsewhere, including to the United States, we rely on an approved safeguard such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (and its UK Extension) or standard contractual clauses. You can ask us for details of the safeguard that applies.

12.How we protect your information

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including multi-factor authentication on our business accounts, encrypted devices, access limited to those who need it, and the security controls of our business platforms. Associates who work with us are bound by confidentiality obligations and our data protection and AI policies.

If a personal data breach occurs that is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify the Jersey Office of the Information Commissioner within 72 hours and tell you without undue delay where required.

13.Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information or restrict how we use it;
  • object to our use of your information, including an absolute right to object to direct marketing;
  • ask us to transfer information you provided to another organisation;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent; and
  • not be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing that significantly affect you.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@momentumjersey.com. We will respond within four weeks. In some circumstances the law allows us to extend this period, and we will tell you if so. We do not normally charge a fee.

14.Complaints

If you have a concern about how we handle your information, please contact us first so we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Jersey Office of the Information Commissioner at jerseyoic.org. If you live in the United Kingdom or the European Union, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office or your local supervisory authority.

15.Changes to this statement

We review this statement at least once a year and whenever our use of personal information changes. The current version will always be available on this page.
